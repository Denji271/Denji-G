Draw & Guess

    Draw with the mouse or your finger · type guesses and press Enter · Esc menu

    How to play

    • Players take turns drawing. The drawer picks one of three words and has to draw it — no letters or numbers!
    • Everybody else types guesses in the chat. The sooner you guess right, the more points you get.
    • The drawer earns points for everybody who guesses. Some letters of the word show up as time runs out.
    • After every player has drawn in every round, the most points wins.
