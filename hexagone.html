Hex-a-gone

WASD or arrows move · Space jump · drag to turn the camera (or Q E), wheel to zoom · Esc menu

How to play

  • The floors are made of hexagons. A moment after anybody steps on one, it shakes, turns red and drops away.
  • Keep moving! Fall through a hole and you land on the floor below — fall through the last floor and you are out.
  • Jump with Space to cross gaps, and bump into the others to steer them towards the holes.
  • Drag with the mouse to look around (or use Q E) and scroll to zoom. W always walks the way the camera looks.
  • The last player standing wins the round. The longer you last, the more points you get.
