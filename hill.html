King of the Hill

WASD move · Space jump · click or J shove · drag to turn the camera (or Q E), wheel to zoom · Esc menu

How to play

  • A glowing ring sits on the hill. Stand in it alone and your clock ticks up — if somebody else is in there too, nobody scores.
  • Every half a minute the ring jumps to a new spot on the hill.
  • Click (or press J) to shove forward and knock the others out of the ring — or off the edge of the island.
  • Fall off and you are back on the island two seconds later. The first to the target time wins, or the most time when the clock runs out.
  • Drag with the mouse to turn the camera (or Q E) and scroll to zoom. W always walks the way the camera looks.
