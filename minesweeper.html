Minesweeper

Level: Beginner Mines left: 0 Time: 0:00

Right-click or long-press to plant a flag. Click a number to open everything around it once its flags are placed.

How to play

  • Open every square that does not hide a mine. A number tells you how many mines touch that square, diagonals included.
  • Your first click is always safe and opens up a patch of the field.
  • Right-click (or long-press, or switch the tap mode) to flag a square you know is a mine.
  • Click a number whose mines are all flagged to open every other square around it at once.
  • Every board can be cleared by logic alone — you never have to guess.
