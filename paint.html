Paint Wars

WASD move · click where to splat (or Space) · drag to turn the camera (or Q E), wheel to zoom · Esc menu

How to play

  • Wherever you walk, the floor turns your colour. Click on the floor to lob a big splat of paint there (or press Space to splat straight ahead).
  • Splats use ink. Your ink fills up slowly — and quickly while you stand on your own colour.
  • You run fast on your own colour and slowly through somebody else's. A splat on the head slows you down for a moment.
  • When the time is up, whoever has covered the most floor wins.
  • Drag with the mouse to turn the camera (or Q E) and scroll to zoom. W always walks the way the camera looks.
