Word Bomb

    Type a word with the letters on the bomb and press Enter · Esc menu

    How to play

    • The bomb shows two or three letters. On your turn, type an English word that contains them — anywhere in the word.
    • A good word passes the bomb on to the next player with new letters. No word can be used twice in a game.
    • The fuse is secret and keeps burning from one player to the next. Whoever holds the bomb when it goes off loses a life.
    • Use every letter in the row under your input box to win a life back.
    • The last player with lives left wins.
