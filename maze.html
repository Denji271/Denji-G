Maze Race

WASD move · drag to look around (or Q E), wheel to zoom · F first person · Esc menu

How to play

  • Everybody starts in the same corner of a hedge maze. Find your way to the exit — the column of light in the far corner shows where it is, but not how to get there.
  • Your own footprints stay on the ground, so you can tell where you have already been.
  • The first one out scores 3 points, then 2, then 1. Once somebody is out, the others have 45 seconds left.
  • Every round the maze is new and a bit bigger. Most points after the last round wins.
  • Drag with the mouse to look around (or use Q E), scroll to zoom, and press F for first-person view. W always walks the way you are looking.
