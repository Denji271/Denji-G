Tank Trouble

↑ ↓ drive · ← → turn (or WASD) · Space fire · Esc menu

How to play

  • Everybody drives a little tank through the same maze. ↑/↓ drive, ←/→ turn, Space fires.
  • Shells bounce off the walls for a few seconds — and they hit their owner just as happily.
  • You can have five shells flying at once.
  • The last tank still rolling gets a point, then a new maze starts. First to the target wins.
