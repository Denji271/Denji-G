Obstacle Course

WASD move · Space jump · Shift or click dive · drag to turn the camera (or Q E), wheel to zoom · Esc menu

How to play

  • Race everybody else to the finish of a wobbly obstacle course: jump the gaps, duck the sweeping bar, dodge the swinging hammers and hop across moving platforms.
  • Floor tiles that turn red are about to drop — and the conveyor belt pulls you backwards.
  • Space jumps. Shift (or a click) is a forward dive — handy for the last bit of a jump.
  • Fall off and you start again from the beginning of that stretch. The first one over the line scores 3 points, then 2, then 1.
  • Drag with the mouse to turn the camera (or Q E) and scroll to zoom. W always runs the way the camera looks.
