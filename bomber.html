How to play

  • Walk with the arrows or WASD and drop a bomb with Space. It goes off after a couple of seconds.
  • The blast runs in a cross. It stops at walls, breaks crates and sets off other bombs — and it catches you too.
  • Crates sometimes hide power-ups: +1 bomb, longer flames and faster feet.
  • The last player standing wins the round.
