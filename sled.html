Sled Race

A D steer · W tuck in · S brake · drag to look around (or Q E), wheel to zoom · Esc menu

How to play

  • Everybody sleds down the same snowy slope. Steer with A D or the arrows; the hill does the rest.
  • Hold W to tuck in: faster, but you turn more slowly. S brakes.
  • Trees stop you dead and snow banks at the edges slow you down. Ramps send you flying!
  • The first one over the finish line scores 3 points, then 2, then 1. Once somebody is down, the rest have 30 seconds.
  • Drag with the mouse to look around (or use Q E) and scroll to zoom — the camera swings back behind your sled when you let go.
