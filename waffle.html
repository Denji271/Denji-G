Click two letters and they swap. Green: in place. Yellow: belongs elsewhere in that word.

How to play

  • The grid holds three across words and three down words, six in total.
  • Their letters are jumbled: click two tiles to swap them.
  • Green means the letter is already home. Yellow means it belongs somewhere else in that row or column. Grey means it is not part of that word.
  • You have fifteen swaps, and the fewer you use, the better.
