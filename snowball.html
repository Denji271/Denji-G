Snowball Fight

WASD move · click where to throw (or Space) · drag to turn the camera (or Q E), wheel to zoom · hold R to scoop snow · Esc menu

How to play

  • Walk with WASD or the arrows. Click on the snow and your snowball flies there in an arc — lead your target! Space throws straight ahead.
  • Drag with the mouse to turn the camera (or Q E) and scroll to zoom — a click without dragging throws. W always walks the way the camera looks.
  • You carry up to five snowballs. Stand still and hold R to scoop up more.
  • Every hit is a point. The third hit freezes a player solid for a few seconds and is worth two extra points.
  • Snow forts and trees stop snowballs — hide behind them. The most points when the time runs out wins.
