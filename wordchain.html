Word Chain

    Type your word and press Enter · Esc menu

    How to play

    • Players take turns. Your word has to start with the last letter of the word before it.
    • Only real English words count, and no word can be used twice in a game.
    • If the clock runs out on your turn you lose a life, and the next player gets the same letter.
    • After every full round the clock gets a little shorter. The last player with lives left wins.
