Minigolf

Point and click to putt — further away hits harder · drag to turn the camera (or Q E), wheel to zoom · ← → aim, hold Space · V whole hole · Esc menu

How to play

  • Everybody plays the same hole at the same time, each with their own ball — the balls pass through each other.
  • Point at the green and click to putt: the further the pointer is from your ball, the harder you hit. With the keyboard, aim with ← → and hold Space to build power.
  • Water or rolling off the course costs a stroke and puts the ball back where it was. Arrows on the green are slopes, and watch out for the windmill!
  • Drag with the mouse to turn the camera (or Q E) and scroll to zoom — a click without dragging putts.
  • Hold V to see the whole hole. The fewest strokes over the course wins.
