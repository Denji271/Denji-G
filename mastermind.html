Mastermind

Guess: 0 / 10 Colours: 6 Length: 4

Filled peg: right colour, right place. Hollow peg: right colour, wrong place.

How to play

  • Work out the order of four hidden colours in ten guesses.
  • There are six colours to choose from and the same colour may appear more than once.
  • A filled peg next to your guess means one colour is in the right place; a hollow peg means the colour is in the code but elsewhere.
  • The pegs never tell you which position they refer to.
  • Keys 1-6 pick a colour, Enter submits the guess.
