Platformer

How to play

Move with ← → or A D, jump with ↑ W or Space. Hold jump to jump higher, ↓ drops through wooden platforms. You can stand on the other players' heads!

Co-op Climb

  • Walls four blocks high are too tall alone — let a friend stand on your head.
  • A coloured switch holds the door of the same colour open, but only while somebody stands on it.
  • Carry the key to the exit to unlock it, then press ↑ at the exit to go in (↓ comes back out). The level is done when everybody is in.

Platform Race

  • First to the chequered flag wins the most points. Flags on the way are checkpoints — spikes or a fall send you back to the last one.

Brawl

  • Hit with J or X. Every hit raises the damage %, and the higher it is the further you fly.
  • Fall off the stage and you lose a life. The last player with lives left wins.

Tag

  • Whoever is it glows yellow and runs a little faster. Touch another player to make them it — they cannot tag you straight back.
  • Every second you spend as it counts against you. After all the rounds, the least time as it wins.
